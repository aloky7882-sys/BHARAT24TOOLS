Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

At BharatOS-Tools, accessible from aloky7882-sys.github.io/BHARATOS-TOOLS, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect your data while you use our free online tools.

🔒 Quick Summary: All our tools run 100% in your browser. We never upload, store, or share your personal files, images, PDFs, or documents. Your privacy is our top priority.

1. Information We Collect

BharatOS-Tools is designed to be a privacy-first platform. We do not require registration, login, or any personal information to use our tools. However, we may collect the following non-personal data:

  • Usage Data: Anonymous statistics about which tools are popular and how visitors navigate our website.
  • Device Information: Browser type, operating system, and screen size to ensure our tools work properly on your device.
  • Cookies: Small text files that help us remember your preferences for a better experience.

2. Local Browser Processing (Zero Data Upload)

All file processing on BharatOS-Tools happens entirely within your web browser. This means:

  • Your images, PDFs, Excel files, and Word documents are never uploaded to any server.
  • All conversions, compressions, and calculations are performed on your own device.
  • Your personal data never leaves your computer or smartphone.
  • We do not store, sell, or share your files with any third party.

3. Cookies and Web Beacons

Like most modern websites, BharatOS-Tools uses cookies to enhance your browsing experience. These cookies help us:

  • Remember your tool preferences and settings.
  • Analyze website traffic to improve our services.
  • Provide personalized content and recommendations.

You can disable cookies anytime through your browser settings, though some features may not work properly.

4. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdSense)

We may use Google AdSense and other third-party advertising partners to display ads on BharatOS-Tools. These partners may use cookies (such as the DART cookie) to serve ads based on your previous visits to our website and other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

5. Analytics and Log Files

BharatOS-Tools may use analytics services to understand how visitors interact with our website. These services collect anonymous data such as:

  • Number of visitors and page views.
  • Geographic location (country-level only).
  • Referral sources and search keywords.
  • Time spent on each tool.

This data is used purely to improve our tools and user experience.

6. Your Data Protection Rights

As a user of BharatOS-Tools, you have the following rights:

  • Right to Access: You can request information about any data we hold about you.
  • Right to Erasure: You can request deletion of any personal data we may have.
  • Right to Object: You can object to processing of your data for advertising purposes.
  • Right to Portability: You can request a copy of your data in a portable format.

7. Children's Privacy

BharatOS-Tools does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. Our tools are safe for use by students of all ages, but parental guidance is recommended for younger users.

8. International Data Transfers

Since all processing happens in your browser, your data does not cross any international borders. Any anonymous analytics data we collect is handled according to standard data protection regulations.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
