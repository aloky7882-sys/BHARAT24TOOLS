About Bharat24Tools

Bharat24Tools is a free collection of everyday online utilities built for students, professionals and small business owners across India. Instead of downloading heavy software or paying for subscriptions, you can compress an image, convert a file to PDF, generate a QR code or calculate your loan EMI directly in your web browser.

Most tools run on your own device using JavaScript, so your files are not uploaded anywhere. A few heavier tools, such as PDF to Excel and Excel to PDF, use a secure server, and your files are deleted automatically shortly after processing.

Frequently asked questions

Is Bharat24Tools free to use?

Yes, every tool is completely free. No signup, no hidden charges and no watermark.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

Most tools run in your browser, so your files stay on your device. A few heavier tools (for example PDF to Excel and Excel to PDF) use a secure server, and your files are deleted automatically shortly after processing.

Do I need to install any software?

No. Everything works in a modern browser on your phone, tablet or computer.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Open a tool, add your file and download the result — no account or email needed.
