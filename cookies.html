Cookies Policy

This is the Cookies Policy for Bharat24Tools. We use cookies to enhance and smooth out your experience while interacting with our free web utilities.

💡 Quick Summary: We use minimal, essential cookies only to improve your experience. We do not sell your data.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files downloaded to your laptop, desktop, or mobile device browser to improve website mechanics. This page describes what information they gather and why we sometimes need to store these preferences.

How We Use Cookies

We use essential cookies to remember adjustments you make inside our web applications (like saving recent calculation strings in the Scientific Calculator History tracker). This data stays local to your device and is never shared.

  • Essential Cookies: Required for core functionality like saving your preferences.
  • Performance Cookies: Help us understand how visitors interact with our tools.
  • Functional Cookies: Remember your settings for a personalized experience.

Third-Party Cookies (AdSense & Analytics)

In the future, we may deploy third-party advertising modules like Google AdSense. These networks automatically deploy DART tracking cookies to read standard metadata variables and serve targeted, relevant ads based on browsing tendencies across the web.

Disabling Cookies

You can prevent the deployment of cookies by adjusting the operational configurations inside your device browser (check your browser's Help menu to learn how). Note that disabling cookie management might degrade the performance trackers of your saved configuration histories.

Your Privacy Matters

At Bharat24Tools, we are committed to protecting your privacy. All file processing happens locally in your browser. Your images, PDFs, and documents never leave your device.
